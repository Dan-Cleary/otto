what otto does and doesn't do

otto does

  • read cloud recording transcripts
  • read ai companion summaries
  • extract code-shaped action items
  • open small draft pull requests
  • attach transcript excerpts to each pr
  • flag low-confidence items for human review

otto never

  • joins your zoom meetings
  • downloads audio or video files
  • auto-merges anything
  • writes data back to zoom
  • creates, modifies, or deletes zoom users
  • ships large or speculative refactors
your engineering agent · always reviewable · always opt-in ottoagent.app
